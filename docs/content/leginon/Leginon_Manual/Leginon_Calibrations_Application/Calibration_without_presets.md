Calibrations can be made without presets although not as conveniently. For each of the
calibration node, the following need to be done before the calibration or even before
acquiring a test image.

## Select the TEM and Camera Configuration

-   Leginon/Some Calibraion Node/Toolbar> Click the "Settings" button ![](images/settings.png).


-   Leginon/Same Calibration Node/Settings> Override Preset = yes


-   Leginon/Same Calibration Node/Settings/Instrument> TEM = your microscope; CCD
    Camera = your CCD camera


-   Leginon/Same Calibration Node/Camera Configuration> Choose according to your
    need. Usually a highly binned configuration that uses the whole camera.


-   Leginon/Same Calibration Node/Camera Configuration> Click OK to save and close
    the window.


-   Leginon/Pixel Size/Same Calibration Node> Acquire a test image with the camera
    tool ![](images/acquire.png) to confirm that the TEM and camera settings are valid.
