## Electron Microscope/Camera with their Controling Computers (Windows)

Microscope need to have the capacity for external control and network connected (See [Network Configuration](/leginon/Leginon_Manual/Complete_Installation/Network_Configuration) section for details on that. Here are known examples of Leginon implementation:

-   FEI: [Tecnai, Polara, Titan Krios](/leginon/Leginon_Manual/Complete_Installation/Installation_on_the_instrument_computers/Installation_on_the_microscope_computer_30/FEI_TecnaiTitan_installation_specifics) (Film recording available with [adaexp.exe](/leginon/Windows_Camera_Package_Requirement#Film_(Only_for_FEI_Tecnai/Titan))
-   FEI [Talos, Arctica](/leginon/Leginon_Manual/Complete_Installation/Installation_on_the_instrument_computers/Installation_on_the_microscope_computer_30/FEI_Talos_installation_specifics) (No photographic film support)
-   JEOL: [1230, 1400, JEM3100FSC, 3200](/leginon/JEOL_installation_specifics)
